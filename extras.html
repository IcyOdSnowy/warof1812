Did You Know?

"Uncle Sam" was inspired by a meat supplier named Sam Wilson who fed the army in 1812.
The Battle of New Orleans was fought AFTER the peace treaty was already signed in Belgium.
The British burned the White House in retaliation for Americans burning the capital of Canada (York).

Campfire Cooking

Soldiers survived on simple, durable rations. Try the most famous one:

Hardtack (Survival Bread)

Ingredients: 2 cups flour, 3/4 cup water, 1 tsp salt.

  1. Mix until you have a stiff dough.
  2. Flatten to 1/2 inch thickness.
  3. Bake at 375°F for 30 mins, flip, and bake for 30 more.
  4. Let it dry for days until it's hard as a rock!

Folk Songs & Ballads

Music was the primary way stories were shared. Listen for these titles in historical archives:

  • 🎵 "The Bold Canadian" — A patriotic song celebrating the defense of Upper Canada.
  • 🎵 "The Girl I Left Behind Me" — A classic marching tune used by both sides.
  • 🎵 "The Star-Spangled Banner" — Written after the bombardment of Fort McHenry.
