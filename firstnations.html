SOVEREIGNNATIONS

The Sovereign Alliance.

Exploring the vital role and profound impact of Indigenous Nations during the conflict.

The Confederacy

Tecumseh’s Dream

The Shawnee leader Tecumseh worked to unite First Nations into a great confederacy. His goal wasn't just to help the British, but to defend Indigenous land and create a sovereign state for his people.

Motivation

Why Support Britain?

Most First Nations saw the Americans as the primary threat to their land. The British were seen as a "lesser of two evils" who promised to respect their borders and sovereignty in exchange for military support.

Tactics

Masters of the Forest

Indigenous warriors were the key to victories at Detroit and Beaver Dams. Their knowledge of the terrain and expert use of psychological warfare often forced larger American armies to surrender in fear.

The Legacy

A Tragic Conclusion

Despite their bravery, the Treaty of Ghent ignored First Nations land rights entirely. After the death of Tecumseh in 1813, the dream of an independent Indigenous state faded, and many communities were forced westward by American expansion.
