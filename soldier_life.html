The Reality of the Frontier

Rations & Survival

Soldiers marched on their stomachs—usually on **Hardtack** (rock-hard biscuits) and **Salt Pork**. Supplies were often so scarce that men went days without eating.

  • The Pay: A British regular earned roughly 1 shilling a day (about $0.20), but much was deducted for clothing and food.
  • Disease: More soldiers died from dysentery and "camp fever" than from enemy fire.

Kit & Equipment

The standard weapon was the **Brown Bess Musket**, accurate only at short ranges. Soldiers carried a 60lb pack containing a blanket, a mess tin, and spare flints.

"It rains almost every day... Our troops are dieing rapidly... no boards for tent floors, very little straw." — Alan Taylor, Historian
