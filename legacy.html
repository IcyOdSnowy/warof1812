Born of Conflict

IDENTITY

A Shared Sense of Self

The war forced English and French Canadians to fight for a shared objective: defending their homes from American invasion. This created the first true sense of a **Canadian community**.

TRAGEDY

The Cost to First Nations

While the British and Americans returned to their pre-war borders, the First Nations lost their greatest champion, **Tecumseh**. The Treaty of Ghent failed to protect Indigenous territory, leading to massive land losses as American expansion pushed westward.

POLITICS

Status Quo Ante Bellum

The Treaty of Ghent (1814) essentially meant "the state of things as they were before the war". No borders changed, but Canada’s path toward becoming a free and independent country under the Crown was solidified.
