The Fields of Glory.

Strategic analysis of the most decisive engagements on the Canadian frontier.

Bluff & Strategy

The Siege of Detroit

The Trick: General Brock and Tecumseh used very clever deceptions to make their force look super massive. They marched the same group of warriors in a circle through a forest opening multiple of times. Terrified, the Americans surrendered without a single shot being fired.

Oct 1812

Queenston Heights

The site of Sir Isaac Brock’s heroic final charge. Though Brock fell, the British and Mohawk warriors trapped the American army against the Niagara cliffs.

Retaliation

Battle of York

US forces burned the public buildings of modern-day Toronto. This "scorched earth" tactic directly led to the British burning Washington D.C. a year later.

Intelligence

Battle of Beaver Dams

After Laura Secord walked 30km to warn of a surprise attack, Indigenous warriors ambushed the Americans in the woods. Nearly the entire US force surrendered to a group they never even saw.

The Bloodiest

Lundy's Lane

Fought into the pitch-black night near Niagara Falls. It was the deadliest battle on Canadian soil, ending the final American attempt to invade the Niagara peninsula.
