Interactive analysis of the frontier's most decisive engagements.

Queenston Heights (Oct 13, 1812)

The first major American invasion. Despite outnumbering the defenders, the US forces were trapped against the Niagara cliffs.

"Victory is certain, and the Americans will be taught a lesson in humility." — Maj. Gen. Isaac Brock
⚔️ Outcome: British Victory 🚩 Key Figure: Sir Isaac Brock

Battle of the Thames (1813)

A tragic turning point for Indigenous resistance. The death of Tecumseh effectively ended his dream of a Pan-Indigenous Confederacy.

Strategically shifted control of the Northwest to the US.

Burning of York (1813)

US forces captured and burned the capital of Upper Canada. This act directly led to the British retaliatory burning of Washington D.C..

Lundy's Lane (July 25, 1814)

The bloodiest battle of the war, fought into the night near Niagara Falls. It was a tactical draw, but a strategic British victory as it halted the final American invasion.

💀 Casualties: ~1,700 total 🌙 Status: Night Engagement
