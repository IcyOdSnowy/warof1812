Decisive Engagements

Queenston Heights (1812)

Strategic Impact: The defense of the Niagara frontier. British regulars and Six Nations warriors repelled the invasion, though Sir Isaac Brock fell in the charge.

Primary Source: "Push on, brave York volunteers!" — Brock's final words.

Battle of the Thames (1813)

The death of Tecumseh here shattered the First Nations confederacy, allowing Americans to secure the Northwest.
