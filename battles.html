THE FIELDS OF GLORY

Queenston Heights (1812)

A pivotal British victory that halted the first major American invasion. While the Americans crossed the Niagara River to take the heights, they were eventually trapped against the cliffs by a combined force of British regulars and Mohawk warriors.

Key Fact: This is where the "Saviour of Upper Canada," Sir Isaac Brock, fell in battle.

The Long Walk: Laura Secord (1813)

Not every battle was won with a musket. After overhearing American plans for a surprise attack at Beaver Dams, Laura Secord walked 30km through treacherous terrain to warn the British forces.
