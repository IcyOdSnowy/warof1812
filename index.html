The War of 1812

Welcome to this interactive website created by two elementary school students. This site contains content in the form of text, pictures, and interactive elements. They document the events on the War of 1812. All information is sourced from trusted sites. The website is divided into 8 modules, each talking about something different in a detailed yet engaging way. There is also a quiz and some bonus content at the end. Feel free to explore however you like!
