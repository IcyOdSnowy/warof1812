The Spark

In 1812, the world was at a breaking point. While Napoleon tore through Europe, the young United States declared war on the British Empire. Why?

Impressment

The British Navy was "pressing" American sailors into service. This was a direct insult to U.S. Sovereignty.

Expansion

American "War Hawks" looked North at Canada, believing it was their destiny to take the land.

Turning Points

1812: Battle of Detroit - Brock and Tecumseh use psychological warfare to win without firing a shot.
1813: Burning of York - American troops burn the capital (modern-day Toronto), leading to a revenge strike on Washington D.C.
1814: Treaty of Ghent - Peace is declared. Borders remain exactly the same, but a new Canadian spirit is born.

The Legends

Tecumseh

The visionary leader who united First Nations to protect their ancestral lands from invasion.

Laura Secord

She braved 30km of swamp and forest to warn the British of a surprise American attack.

Sir Isaac Brock

The "Saviour of Upper Canada" whose bravery at Queenston Heights cost him his life but saved a colony.
