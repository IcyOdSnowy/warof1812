Key Turning Points

1812

War Declared

The United States declares war on Britain, targeting Upper and Lower Canada.

Identity

Shaping a Nation

Living through the invasion made colonists realize they were not Americans. This shared pride in defending their homes against the [Burning of York](https://americanacorner.com) laid the groundwork for a unified Canada.

Unity

French-English Cooperation

At the [Battle of Châteauguay](https://thecanadianencyclopedia.ca), French-Canadian Voltigeurs and English troops united to stop the American advance on Montreal.

Loyalty

Bonds to the Crown

The war strengthened loyalty to Britain. The monarchy represented order and stability compared to the "republican chaos" seen south of the border.

The Legacy

Why We Remember Today

Without this war, Canada likely wouldn't exist as a separate nation. It defined the borders and ensured that North America would remain a place of two distinct cultures.
