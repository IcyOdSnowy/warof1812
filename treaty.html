Module 07

The Treaty of Ghent.

Peace on Christmas Eve, 1814. A diplomatic end to a brutal frontier war.

The Agreement

Signed: December 24, 1814

Location: Ghent, Belgium

Parties: Great Britain and the United States

"Status Quo Ante Bellum"

Terms of Peace

The treaty returned relations to their pre-war state. Neither side gained or lost land.

  • Immediate Cessation: An end to all fighting on land and sea.
  • Restoration: All conquered forts and islands were returned.
  • Borders: The 1811 boundary lines remained unchanged.
  • Silence: Major causes like impressment were not addressed.

Impact on Canada

Survival

Ensured the Canadas remained British territory and were not annexed.

Identity

Fostered a new shared pride between French and English Canadians.

The Militia Myth

The belief that local citizens, not British regulars, won the war.

Indigenous Betrayal

Indigenous allies were excluded; their lands remained unprotected.
